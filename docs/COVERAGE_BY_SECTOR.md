for private schools it's __%?"* — with the per-sector split, because coverage here is
**not missing-at-random** and a single overall number hides the structural gap.

## How to read this

- **CRDC signals** (AP participation, dual enrollment, SAT/ACT testtaker rate, graduation
  rate, FRL) are **public-school-only by federal design** → ~0% private. This is not a
  matching failure; the data does not exist for private schools. Any deliverable that
  leans on CRDC has a built-in private-sector blind spot (~13,800 schools).
- **NU analytics** (AP exam score, AP tests taken/take-rate, SAT participation/score,
  neighborhood context) track **NU's recruiting universe** — ~40% of public and ~17% of
  private schools. Treat "no NU data" as informative (not on NU's radar), not random.
- **ACT** (ISBE) is Illinois-only and thin (~3% public) — usable for an IL pass, not nationally.
- The public **testtaker rate reaches 91%** — this is the ~80%+ figure the client
  recalled; the corresponding private number is 0% (CRDC), so private-school test
  participation has to come from the thinner NU field (~17%).

**Implication for the rigor model:** private-school tiers rest almost entirely on the
NU analytics block (AP/SAT scores, ~17% coverage) plus the IB flag — they cannot use
any CRDC signal. State this prominently wherever private-school tiers are reported.
